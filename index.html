How to play

Use your physical Scrabble board. Each player gets 12 turns, split into three rounds of four turns per player. Draw seven tiles at the beginning and replenish after each turn.

  1. Play a word on the physical board.
  2. On the iPad, tap the starting square of the entire word, including letters already on the board.
  3. Type the complete word and choose Across or Down. For any newly placed blank tile, list its 1-based position in the word.
  4. The app checks placement, computes main-word and crossing-word points, applies premium squares only to newly placed tiles, and awards the 50-point bingo automatically when seven new tiles are played.
  5. Review the suggested romantic/suggestive bonus; agree together or change it. Tap Play Word.
  6. Read the challenge, do it or skip it, then tap Next Player.

First word must cover the center star. Later words must connect to existing tiles. All new tiles in a turn must be in one line. This app checks board placement and scoring, not dictionary validity; agree on acceptable words together. Challenges and adult bonuses are optional. Either player can pass at any time.

Pass/exchange: This version records word placements only. If you need to pass or exchange tiles, use the Skip Turn button below (no score), and exchange physical tiles yourself.

At the end of 12 turns each, the highest total wins. This fixed-turn house game does not deduct leftover tile values.
